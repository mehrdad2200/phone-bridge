# Code of Conduct

This project is for everyone who wants to make controlling a phone from a PC (and back) easier — whatever their country, language, gender, age, ability, background or experience. English and Persian are both first-class here.

## What we expect

- Be kind and patient. Assume good faith; most problems are misunderstandings or missing information.
- Be constructive: criticise code and ideas, not people.
- Welcome newcomers and people whose first language isn't the one being used; it's fine to write in English or فارسی.
- Respect privacy: don't post other people's personal data, pairing codes, QR images or access links.
- Keep discussions on topic and free of harassment, discrimination, personal attacks, sexual or violent content, and spam.

## Scope

This applies to issues, pull requests, discussions, reviews and any space where someone represents the project.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit, hide or remove comments, close threads, and block people who don't follow these rules. To report a problem, contact the maintainers privately (via the repository owner's GitHub profile contact, or a private security advisory if it can't wait). Reports are handled in confidence and with care.

If a maintainer is the problem, contact another maintainer or GitHub Support.

*Adapted in spirit from widely used community codes such as the Contributor Covenant.*
